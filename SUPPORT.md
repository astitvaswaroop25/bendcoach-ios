# BendCoach support

Questions, bug reports and ideas: [open an issue](https://github.com/astitvaswaroop25/bendcoach-ios/issues) on GitHub.

## Common questions

**My stub-ups come out long or short.**
Hand benders differ slightly from their published take-up. On the **Bend** tab, tap your bender, then **Add a bender**. Tap **Measure it with a test bend** and make one bend on scrap. BendCoach uses your measured take-up from then on.

**How do I get BendCoach Pro back on a new phone?**
Sign in with the same Apple ID, then tap **Restore purchases** on the Pro screen or in **Settings → BendCoach Pro**. Pro is a one-time purchase, so restoring it is free.

**The level reads the wrong angle.**
Lay the phone's long edge on the straight part of the conduit and tap **Zero here** before you bend. Then lay it on the bent section, pointing the same way.

**Offset numbers don't match my bender's chart.**
Settings → **Offset multipliers** switches between exact trigonometry and the rounded chart numbers printed on most benders (6, 3.9, 2.6, 2, 1.4, 1.2).

BendCoach is a guide. Check marks against your bender's own markings, and make a test bend when you start with a new bender.
