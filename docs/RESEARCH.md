# Market research (November 2026)

## Bottom line

Electricians clearly pay for conduit-bending apps. The leaders charge $3–10 and have thousands
of ratings. But "a correct calculator" is easy to copy now: many new single-calculator apps
shipped in 2025–26 and have few or no ratings. BendCoach has to win on something else:
**clarity at the bender** (numbered marks, one step at a time) and a **built-in level** that
other apps charge extra for or don't have.

## Gaps BendCoach goes after

1. **Step clarity.** People bend one mark at a time with dirty gloves on. Big numbered marks, which
   bender mark to use (arrow, star or notch), and when to rotate the pipe.
2. **Level included.** The phone sits on the pipe, loads each step's target angle, and buzzes and
   beeps on target. Conduit Bender Elite sells this separately.
3. **Apprentices.** A new group of apprentices arrives every year. "Guided" mode and plain-English
   steps teach as they go, and QuickBend reviews show that first-years value visualizing the bend.
4. **Trust.** Every formula is documented with sources (docs/MATH.md), and the multiplier mode can
   match the chart printed on the user's bender.

## Pricing decision

The free tier covers stub-ups and offsets, the most common daily bends, so the app gets
downloaded and reviewed. Pro is a one-time **$7.99** (placeholder in `BendCoach.storekit`),
priced between QuickBend ($6.99) and Conduit Bender Elite ($8.99 + $1.99 level). There's no
subscription, which reviews and listings in this category treat as a selling point.
