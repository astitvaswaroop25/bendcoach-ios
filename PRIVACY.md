# BendCoach privacy policy

Effective November 12, 2026

BendCoach does not collect any information about you.

- **No account.** You never sign up or sign in.
- **No analytics, ads or tracking.** The app contains no code that collects usage data or follows you across apps.
- **Your data stays on your device.** Saved bends, custom benders and settings are stored only on your iPhone or iPad and are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.
- **The level.** The level reads your device's motion sensor only while it is on screen. Readings stay on the device and are not saved.
- **Purchases.** BendCoach Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment, and the developer never sees your payment details. [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) covers that purchase.
- **Children.** BendCoach collects no information from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.

Questions: [open an issue](https://github.com/astitvaswaroop25/bendcoach-ios/issues) on GitHub.
